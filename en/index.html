Welcome

Orkide Thai Restaurant is a relaxed spot for lunch, dinner or takeaway.

We only use fresh ingredients to prepare every dish with love. Enjoy our varied menu of Thai and Chinese dishes, along with a delicious sushi experience.

The restaurant is family-owned and fully licensed, so you can complement your meal with a glass of wine or a nice cold beer.
